Gallery

A collection of snippets, ideas, images, and little things that made my day.

First successful PCR confirming Acinetobacter baumannii.

Three months of painstaking lab work, followed by a long wait for the primers. Then came this tiny band, matching the positive control. I was so excited that I showed it to everyone and proudly announced, “This is going into my submission.”

Came for the flowers, stayed for the photo.

I was taking photos of flowers when this bee suddenly showed up. Naturally, I assumed it wanted a photo too. Took a few shots, and then it flew away. I suppose that was its entire modelling career.

The aurora was better in person.

I met this guy randomly while he was visiting Umeå from Peru, and somehow we ended up looking for the aurora together. Neither of us had seen one up close before. While the aurora was great, the photo, unfortunately, had to work with my camera.

The photographer became the subject.

Someone took a photo of me while I was trying to take a photo of my mom. Is this considered metaphotography or am I just overthinking a photo?

Taking in the view. And my insignificance.

I actually do not know who took this photo because there were about 18 of us on that tour. I was just sitting and looking at those "mountains" from below when I suddenly realized how small I am in the universe. Apparently, that is all it took to trigger an existential crisis.

My kitchen-window philosopher.

This tree stood right outside my kitchen in Umeå. I watched it go through summer and winter, looking slightly different every time, but somehow always standing and glowing in its own way. At some point, I started feeling like it was doing a better job at life than I was.

A microscopic mystery.

I saw this little heart-shaped thing under a microscope. I actually do not know what it is. If you can figure it out, let me be part of the unraveling too.
