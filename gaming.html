Gaming

Growing up, video games were a big part of my life. This is a small record of the games I played, the ones that stayed with me, and the things I enjoyed about them.

All-time favorites

Anno 1800

I have always enjoyed city-building games like SimCity and Cities XL, but Anno 1800 takes it further. You build cities across different regions while managing trade, diplomacy, propaganda, and war. I have played most of the other Anno series (except Pax Romana), but Anno 1800 takes the cake for me because it combines the satisfaction of building cities with the complexity of running an expanding empire.

Star Wars: Empire at War

The idea of becoming an interstellar civilization fascinated me growing up. As a Star Wars fan, I often wondered how societies might develop once a species expanded beyond its home planet. Since people are bound to fight one another,this game along with the movies and tv series made it possible to imagine what warfare might look like on a galactic scale.

Games I played professionally

League of Legends

I discovered this game by accident. As I became skilled, I ended up playing on several teams in different roles and won a few tournaments here and there. I even travelled to Sri Lanka for a tournament in 2018. We did not win, but it was a fun experience nonetheless.

Counter Strike: Source

My friends introduced me to this game while we played at gaming zones. Since we were always competing with each other, my skills improved. I played in a few tournaments here and there, and it was mostly just a fun, goofball experience. Whether this counts as playing professionally is still under review.
